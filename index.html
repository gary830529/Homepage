About Me

My name is Hsiang-Yang (Gary) Shyu and I am a postdoctoral research scholar working with Dr. Daniel Yeh in the Membrane Biotechnology Lab at the University of South Florida.

My work sits at the intersection of wastewater process engineering and machine learning. As a postdoc, I contribute to the Honu Hub, a solar-powered onsite wastewater recycling system, where I built its data logging, remote monitoring, and control system and am integrating AI into its design and monitoring.

During my Ph.D., I led the field evaluation of the NEWgenerator™, a non-sewered sanitation system that recovers clean water, nutrients, and energy from wastewater, and developed machine learning soft sensors for real-time water quality monitoring. My expertise also includes membrane-based separation, nutrient recovery with advanced adsorbents, and dynamic process modeling.

I earned my Ph.D. in Environmental Engineering from USF in 2024. I am honored to be the 14th doctoral graduate from the Membrane Biotechnology Lab!
